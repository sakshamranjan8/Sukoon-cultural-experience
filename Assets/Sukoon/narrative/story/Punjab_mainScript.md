Scene 1 – The City
Visual: The person wakes to a shrill alarm. Coffee maker gurgles. Taxi honks through traffic. Office desk, unread mails piled on screen. Night falls. Taxi returns home amid honking horns. Person collapses on bed, stares at the ceiling, exhales, sleeps.

Interactivity: None

Narration (The Person):

Another day.

Another cup of coffee.

Buzzing horns. A city full of strangers. A phone full of notifications.

So many people. So little warmth.

The world — it feels distant.


Scene 2 – The Delivery
Visual: Morning light spills through the window. The doorbell rings. The person shuffles to the door, coffee in one hand. A parcel sits on the doorstep — brown paper, a wax seal, tied with jute.

Interactivity: Tap the door to open it.

Person:

Who even sends letters anymore?

(Picks up the package. Carries it inside. Places it on the bed. Begins to unwrap it.)


Scene 3 – The Book Opens
Visual: Inside the paper sits a beautiful brown leather book. On its cover, a single word: Destiny.

Interactivity: Tap the book to open it.

The book opens to the first page. The words appear slowly, as if being written by an unseen hand.

Text on the page:

"The book always finds those who need it the most. Follow the path — a soulful awakening awaits you."

Person (whispers):

This has to be a joke... right?


Scene 4 – The State Index
Visual: The person turns the page. An index of Indian states appears. 

Interactivity: Tap the state where you want your expedition. (As of now, only Punjab is enabled.)

Narration:

You run your finger down the list. One name glows.

Index of States:

Punjab – Where love, generosity and courage flows like its rivers. (Active)

Kerala – Where the monsoon writes poetry. (Locked)

Bengal – Where art breathes and poetry sings. (Locked)

Tamil Nadu – Where temples speak in stone. (Locked)

Rajasthan – Where deserts bloom with courage. (Locked)

Nagaland – Where the hills remember every song. (Locked)

(The person taps Punjab. The book grows warm in their hands.)

Person:

Punjab...


Scene 5 – Meher Appears
Visual: The book opens on its own to a different page. A mirror image of a woman appears within it — Meher, the guardian. Calm eyes. Warm smile. She is looking directly at the person.

Meher:

I am Meher. Your companion and guide for this journey.

The book found you for a reason. You do not have to understand it yet. You only have to follow.

Punjab will not fix you. It will simply remind you what it means to be whole again.

So go. Board the train.


Scene 6 – The Train
Visual: The person is now at a railway station. A train pulls in. They board. The train begins to move. Through the window — wheat and rice fields, lush and blooming, rolling past like a slow breath.

Interactivity: None (cinematic with scenic pan).

Meher:

Look outside. Really look.

Punjab is much like its fields — generous and content, yet sturdy and courageous.

You have spent so long rushing. Here, you must pause. Life is an experience, after all. And experiences are meant to be felt, not survived.

A family will welcome you when you arrive. They do not know you, but they will welcome you anyway. That is the spirit of Punjab — they hold human connection above all else.

Watch them. Learn from them.


Scene 7 – Arrival in Punjab
Visual: The train slows. The person steps onto the platform. A family waits — a mother, a father, and their young daughter holding a garland of marigolds. All three are smiling.

Family (together):

Welcome. We have been awaiting you.

Mother:

Meher always knows who needs the village — and whom the village needs. Come. Let us take you home.

Father (with a warm laugh):

You must be tired. The city takes more than it gives, no? Here, you will get it back.

Daughter (holding out the marigolds):

These are for you. They bloom every morning, no matter what. Like us.

Meher:

Welcome to Punjab, traveler.

Your journey begins here.
